Spacecraft fire-safety research

A smoke alarm only helps if it goes off.

Artemis II commander Reid Wiseman reported a smoke alarm on the return trip, about 80,000 miles from Earth. Silent Alarm asks the quieter question: what if the smoke is there and the alarm stays silent?

On the ISS

Two findings from NASA's SAME tests

The tested station smoke detector often stayed quiet for smoke from two common spacecraft materials.

About two-thirds

Teflon smoke tests

In about two-thirds of Teflon smoke tests, the tested station smoke detector would have stayed below its alarm threshold.

Qualifier: under the conditions studied in NASA's SAME tests on the ISS.

Source: SAME detector study, Figure 9

Over half

Kapton smoke tests

In over half of Kapton smoke tests, the tested station smoke detector would have stayed below its alarm threshold.

Qualifier: under the conditions studied in NASA's SAME tests on the ISS.

What Silent Alarm does
