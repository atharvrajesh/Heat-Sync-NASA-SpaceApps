Silent Alarm

A smoke alarm only helps if it goes off.

Artemis II commander Reid Wiseman reported a smoke alarm on the return trip, about 80,000 miles from Earth. In NASA's SAME tests on the ISS, the tested station smoke detector would have stayed below its alarm threshold in about two-thirds of Teflon smoke tests and over half of Kapton smoke tests, under the conditions studied.

Silent Alarm is for spacecraft fire-safety researchers. It ranks NASA's microgravity combustion investigations for your question, summarizes the top matches with AI, shows the detector evidence with its limits, and proposes the next test to run.

For lunar habitats, a key question is how smoke detectors respond at the Moon's gravity. Flame in Freefall challenge background

    Proposed lunar follow-up Compare the same smoke detector and selected material in microgravity and simulated lunar gravity. Match airflow, smoke aging time, oxygen concentration, and pressure. Record the detector signal and whether and when it alarms.

        How smoke particles change as they age

        NASA's SAME flight tests reported the arithmetic mean smoke particle diameter at baseline and after aging. All bars use the same 0 to 450 nm scale.

          The reported mean diameter was larger after aging for all four materials. Matching smoke age would make a follow-up detector comparison easier to interpret. The detector study describes the alarm thresholds separately.
