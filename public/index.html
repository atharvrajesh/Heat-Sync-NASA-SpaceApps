      How smoke particles change as they age

      NASA's SAME flight tests reported the arithmetic mean smoke particle diameter at baseline and after 720 seconds of aging. All bars use the same 0 to 450 nm scale.

      • Kapton
        Baseline 105 nm
        Aged 186 nm
      • Teflon
        Baseline 205 nm
        Aged 273 nm
      • Cotton lamp wick
        Baseline 227 nm
        Aged 308 nm
      • Silicone rubber
        Baseline 257 nm
        Aged 422 nm

      The reported mean diameter was larger after aging for all four materials. Matching smoke age would make a follow-up detector comparison easier to interpret. The detector study describes the alarm thresholds separately.
